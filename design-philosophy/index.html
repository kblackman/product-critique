philosophy

What I believe about design, and how it plays out in practice.

Four working principles, tested most recently while building Attribution's Recommendation Cards at Freshpaint.

Scroll
01 Evidence

Data over assumption, every time.

I don't start with what seems obviously true. I start with data, whatever hard evidence I can get my hands on: usage numbers, experiment results, support tickets, sales calls, competitive research, anything that shows me what's actually happening rather than what I assume is happening. When a choice as small as a flooring option can quietly move a budget, assumption is an expensive place to start.

02 Transparency

Show your work.

If something is going to shape a person's decision, whether that's an AI-generated suggestion or a number on a dashboard, they deserve to see where it came from. Show the reasoning, not just the result

03 Ownership

Ownership doesn't end at launch.

Shipping is the midpoint, not the finish line. I stay with what I build long enough to know if it's actually working, and to fix it if it isn't. That matters even more when the thing you're optimizing for is retention, not just adoption.

04 Judgment

AI is a partner, not the main brain.

It speeds up how fast I can think, prototype, and ship. It doesn't replace good judgment. I decide what to keep, what to edit, and what to reject, every time.
