Shared rules

120×120 box, 6 stroke, flat butt terminals, 45° and 90° angles only. Radius varies by direction (noted under each). Prior phases persist at 20% amber so the mark accumulates across the card stack.

My pick

A+ at three rings. It keeps A's resolve and doubles the accumulation, and the glyph never drops below 48px on the page. Plain A if you want the mark to survive any size; C if you want the k's amber as the through-line.

Watch out

A+ at five rings is a poster mark, not a card mark — see the size test. B risks reading as a stray letter next to the real logotype in the header. D is the busiest at small sizes — three arches plus a baseline lose definition below about 40px.
